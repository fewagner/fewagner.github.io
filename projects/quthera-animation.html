Conversion. A dark photon γ′ passes the shielding and converts at the Cu hemisphere into a terahertz photon γ, emitted normal to the surface.
Focusing. Normal emission from the whole surface converges on the dish centre, where the Si lens couples it onto the qubit chip.
Absorption. The pier of the qubit island acts as a double-slot antenna and delivers the photon to the Josephson junction.
Pair breaking. The photon (hν > 2Δ) breaks a Cooper pair delocalized across the junction, leaving one quasiparticle on each side.
Parity flip. The island charge changes by one electron (ng → ng + ½), so the resonance jumps by ε and the probe transmission steps up.
