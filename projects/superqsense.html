SuperQSense

SuperQSense is a project exploring the use of superconducting qubits as sensors for fundamental physics. The extraordinary sensitivity of superconducting circuits to single quasiparticles and microwave photons opens new avenues for detecting faint signals from processes beyond the Standard Model — including the direct detection of dark matter.
